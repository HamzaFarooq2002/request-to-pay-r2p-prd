Designed a comprehensive Product Requirements Document (PRD) for introducing Request 2 Pay (R2P) in the Pakistani digital payments ecosystem — a feature currently underutilized despite strong market demand.

The product enables the payee to initiate a structured payment request, which is sent as a secure notification to the payer. Upon authorization, funds are directly debited — eliminating friction, trust gaps, and manual coordination in payments.

🎯 Problem Identified

Pakistan’s digital payments landscape is saturated with standard P2P transfers, but lacks a structured request-based payment mechanism. This creates inefficiencies in:

Group payments (restaurants, shared expenses)

E-commerce (trust deficit leading to Cash on Delivery)

B2B transactions and invoicing

Late bill reminders and payment follow-ups

🚀 Product Vision

Position R2P as a new payment use case that:

Increases overall transaction volume

Enhances payment convenience for Gen Z users

Reduces reliance on Cash on Delivery in e-commerce

Enables seamless bill splitting and structured B2B/B2P payments

Improves payment trust through payer authorization

👥 Target Personas

Gen Z Users
Enable instant group payment collection (e.g., restaurant bills of PKR 5,000) without sharing account credentials in group chats.

E-commerce Merchants
Allow customers to authorize payments at delivery — reducing COD dependency caused by trust issues.

Businesses (B2B / B2P)
Structured invoice-based payment requests and automated reminders for better cash flow predictability.

🏗 Core Use Cases

Consumer Banking

B2B Transactions

Business-to-Person (B2P)

Bill Payments & Payment Reminders

⚙️ Key Constraints Considered

Interoperability across banks and payment networks

User trust and authorization controls

Seamless integration within existing banking apps

📊 Strategic Impact

R2P strengthens digital payment infrastructure by:

Increasing payment volume and frequency

Improving merchant cash flow

Reducing reconciliation complexity

Addressing behavioral and trust gaps in Pakistani fintech
